SECTION 8 — ORDERING PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steps that actually exist on the website:

1. Browse products — Products page ("/products"); search, filter by category,
   sort. Optionally open a product's details page.
2. Select product & quantity — On the product details page, choose quantity.
3. Add to cart OR Buy Now —
       • "Add to Cart" adds the item to the customer's cart (cart is stored on
         the server per user account).
       • "Buy Now" takes the customer straight to checkout for just that product
         (a separate flow that does not clear the cart).
4. Review cart — Cart page ("/cart") shows items, quantities, subtotal, delivery
   charge and GST; customer can change quantities or remove items.
5. Proceed to checkout — Cart page → "Proceed to Checkout" (or Buy Now). Requires
   being logged in; guests are redirected to login first.
6. Enter delivery address — full name, email, phone, address line 1 (required),
   address line 2 (optional), city, state, pincode, country (default India).
7. Select payment method — Cash on Delivery (COD) or Online (Razorpay:
   UPI / Card / NetBanking).
8. Complete payment —
       • COD: place order directly.
       • Online: opens Razorpay checkout; after payment, the payment signature
         is verified by the backend before the order is confirmed.
9. Order confirmation — Order-success page ("/order-success") shows the order ID,
   payment summary, delivery address, and delivery progress. Confirmation email
   and WhatsApp notifications are sent.
10. Track order — My Orders page ("/my-orders") shows order status and lets the
    customer cancel while the order is cancellable (see SECTION 13).

SECTION 9 — CART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Behavior:
    - The cart is server-side and tied to the logged-in user's account (cart
      lives in the User document).
    - Only logged-in customers can have a cart. When not logged in, the cart is
      empty; adding to cart / checkout redirects to login.
    - Cart items are fetched automatically when the user logs in.

Add to cart:
    - From product cards (Products page / Home best-sellers) and the product
      details page. Adding shows a "Cart Updated" toast.

Quantity:
    - Quantity can be increased/decreased on the Cart page. Minimum quantity is
      1 (cannot go below 1).

Remove:
    - Items can be removed individually with the trash icon.

Totals shown on the Cart page:
    - Subtotal (sum of item prices × quantities).
    - Delivery Charges — FREE if subtotal is 0 or ≥ free-delivery threshold
      (default ₹500); otherwise ₹50.
    - GST — computed as subtotal × tax percentage (default 0%, so normally
      absent/₹0).
    - Final total = subtotal + delivery + GST.

Checkout transition:
    - "Proceed to Checkout" navigates to "/checkout". If items remain in the
      cart after an order is placed (CART flow), the cart is cleared on the
      backend.

Persistence:
    - Cart is persisted in the database per user, so it survives page reloads and
      is available across devices once logged in.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 10 — CHECKOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mode:
    - Two checkout modes, strictly separated: CART (from the cart) and BUY_NOW
      (single product from a "Buy Now" button). Buy Now checkout only ever
      contains the chosen product and never touches the cart.

Required customer information:
    - Shipping address: fullName, email, phone (10-digit validation), addressLine1,
      addressLine2 (optional), city, state, pincode (validated), country
      (defaults to India).
    - The logged-in user's name/email/phone are pre-filled; a saved address can
      be loaded if the user previously saved one.

Order summary shown:
    - Items, Subtotal, Delivery Charges, GST (if any), Total Amount.

Payment options:
    - Cash on Delivery (COD).
    - Online — Razorpay ("UPI / Card / NetBanking", "Razorpay 256-bit encrypted
      checkout (GPay, PhonePe, Cards)").

Pricing / taxes:
    - Subtotal from product prices.
    - Delivery charge: ₹50 unless subtotal ≥ ₹500 (then FREE).
    - GST: based on configured tax percentage (default 0%).
    - Total = subtotal + delivery + GST.

Confirmation:
    - COD: order created immediately via POST /api/orders, then success page.
    - Online: order is only created after Razorpay payment is verified by the
      backend (POST /api/payment/verify).

Buy Now specifics:
    - "Buy Now" from a product card/details carries that single product into
      checkout and purchases only that product without affecting the cart.

SECTION 11 — PAYMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Payment gateway:          Razorpay (online). Cash on Delivery also supported.
Payment methods offered:  COD (Cash on Delivery) and Online (Razorpay — UPI,
                          Cards, NetBanking; the checkout copy names GPay and
                          PhonePe as examples).
Online checkout flow:
    1. Customer confirms the order summary and picks "UPI / Card / NetBanking".
    2. Frontend loads the Razorpay checkout SDK.
    3. Backend creates a Razorpay order after verifying products and stock
       server-side (POST /api/payment/create-order).
    4. Razorpay payment modal opens for the exact order amount (INR).
    5. On success, the frontend sends the Razorpay order/payment/signature to
       the backend, which verifies the signature (POST /api/payment/verify)
       before the order is marked Paid and created.
    6. Order confirmation page + email/WhatsApp notifications follow.
Payment statuses on orders: Pending, Paid, Failed, Refunded.
Failed/declined payment behavior: the checkout shows an error message (e.g.,
"Payment failed") and the customer can retry or switch to COD. Payment method
options include a fallback message telling the customer they may choose Cash on
Delivery if the gateway fails to load.
Refunds:
    - Online payments (Razorpay) can be refunded when an order is cancelled —
      refunds are processed back to the original payment method.
    - Refund Policy page: Razorpay refunds within 5–10 business days after
      approval; you receive an email when the refund is initiated.
    - Footer Refund Policy modal: online refunds within 5–7 business days; COD
      refunds by bank transfer within 3–5 business days.
    - Exchange is offered as an alternative to refunds in eligible cases.
Security copy:           "100% secure checkout with encrypted payment gateway";
                         "Razorpay 256-bit encrypted checkout".
Credentials:             NOT included here (Razorpay key/secret and all other
                         secrets live in server environment variables — see
                         SECTION 23 for what was deliberately excluded).

SECTION 12 — CUSTOMER ACCOUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Signup:
    - Route "/signup". Fields: full name, email, password, confirm password.
    - Password must be at least 6 characters. Passwords must match.
    - New accounts are created with role "user". Success → logged in.
Login:
    - Route "/login". Email + password. Valid email format required.
    - On success a JWT token is stored (7-day expiry) and the user is redirected
      (default: Home; otherwise to the page they were trying to visit).
Authentication:
    - JWT-based, token kept in localStorage.
    - The entire storefront (Home, About, Products, Gallery, Contact, Cart,
      Checkout, My Orders, Enquiry, Product Details) is behind a login wall
      (ProtectedRoute) — users must be logged in to browse.
    - Login/Signup pages redirect already-authenticated users to Home.
User profile:
    - Profile data: full name, email, phone, saved shipping address, role.
    - Account functionality is minimal on the customer side: there is no
      dedicated "profile settings" page in the public storefront. Address is
      entered/saved during checkout.
My Orders:
    - Route "/my-orders". Lists the logged-in user's orders with status, dates,
      items, and a detail view; supports order cancellation (see SECTION 13).
Logout:
    - Available from the navbar dropdown. Clears the session token.
Admin accounts:
    - Admins (role "admin") have a separate panel at "/admin/*". Admins are
      created via a seed script; customers cannot self-register as admins.
    - The admin area is not customer-facing and should not be described in
      detail to customers.
